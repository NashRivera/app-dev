# Rambo

## Movie Overview

### Genre: Action, Adventure, & Thriller

*Rambo* tells the story of John Rambo, a former Green Beret soldier with excellent combat and survival skills. He faces dangerous enemies and difficult situations while using his military training to survive. Throughout the movie, he shows courage, strength, and determination.

"To survive a war, you have to become a warrior." — John Rambo

1. **First Blood:** Rambo faces unfair treatment from a small-town sheriff and fights to survive.
2. **Rambo: First Blood Part II:** Rambo returns to Vietnam on a mission to rescue American prisoners of war.
3. **Rambo III:** Rambo travels to Afghanistan to rescue his former commander, Colonel Trautman.

- John Rambo

- Sheriff Will Teasle
